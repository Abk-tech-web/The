Who he is

The Little Elon

A kid in a thrifted jacket, wearing safety glasses rigged with a hand-soldered circuit board, a battery pack, and a fiber-optic cable he probably wasn't supposed to take home from the lab. Nobody told him to build it. He built it anyway.

That's the whole thesis of $INVENTO: the people quietly duct-taping something together in the corner are usually a few steps ahead of everyone waiting for permission.

Vision · Ideas · Impact

What we're actually optimizing for

Vision

A token treated like a product, not a slogan — with a roadmap you can check on instead of one you have to take on faith.

Ideas

Small, testable improvements over big unverifiable promises. Every claim on this site is something you'll be able to check against the live dashboard.

Impact

A community that measures itself by what got built and shipped, not just by how loud it got on a given day.

Roadmap

Where this is headed

  1. Phase 01 — Foundation

    Build in public

    Website, community channels, and this dashboard shipped before the contract does — so there's something to verify against from day one.

  2. Phase 02 — Launch

    Contract deployment

    Once CONTRACT_ADDRESS is set in config.js, the dashboard automatically starts pulling live price, liquidity, and security data — no manual updates required.

  3. Phase 03 — Growth

    Community & listings

    Grow the Telegram community, pursue additional DEX visibility, and keep security and liquidity information public and current.

  4. Phase 04 — Build

    What comes next

    Future tools and utilities get proposed and voted on by the community that's actually here — not decided behind closed doors.
